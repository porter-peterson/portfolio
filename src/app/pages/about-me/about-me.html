About Me Software Developer

Hi, my name is Porter Peterson and I have been working in software development for over four years, while simultaneously pursuing a degree in Computer Science at Western Governor's University.

Since starting my education and degree, I have always been passionate about creating and building software and products with focuses on improving the lives of those who use it. Various interfaces of technology and AI have become such pervasive factors in our lives, and I see them as a tool that can be utilized in helping others.

When faced with a challenge or problem, I appreciate the ways in which programming allows me to use critical thinking skills as well as multiple avenues to try and create something others can use or access. I have confidently worked with JavaScript and Typescript for the past four years and find them to be accessible and flexible for the work I strive to do. I also have experience building scalable web applications in the cloud.

Outside of the IDE, my life is defined by the same precision I bring to my code. I am an avid fly fisherman and fly tyer. Hobbies that have taught me the value of patience, observation, and the importance of choosing the right 'tool' for the specific environment. I find that the most rewarding challenges are the ones that require critical thinking and a touch of creativity. These pursuits keep me grounded and constantly remind me that the best solutions, whether in a stream or a cloud-based application, require a deep understanding of the ecosystem they live in.
